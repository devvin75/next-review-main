Dynamic rendering.
1.)Real-Time Data
--Allows the application to display real-time or frequently updated data.

2.)User-Specific content
--it's easier to serve personalized content, such as dashboards or user profiles,
  and update the data based on user interaction.
  
3.)Request Time Information
--dynamic rendering allow you to access information that can ONLY be known
  at request time, such as cookies or the URL search parameters.
